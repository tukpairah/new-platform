Dodge fight icons (write the paths in config.js under games.dodge.art)
Use square PNG files with a transparent background (WEBP, SVG, JPG and GIF also work).
Recommended sizes:  player 128x128   boss 512x512   bullets 64x64   portrait 256x256
The picture is only decoration: the real hitbox is set in config.js (player.hitRadius, bulletHit).
